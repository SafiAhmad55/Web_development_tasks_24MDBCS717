It is websites which deals with the students of uet mardan.The student will be able to discover university events,workshops,sports activites and campus program.

About Our Portal

The UET Mardan Student Activities Portal helps students stay updated about university events, workshops, sports competitions, and cultural activities. It provides information about upcoming events, schedules, and registration so students can participate and make the most of university life.
